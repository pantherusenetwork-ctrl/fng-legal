About

FNG Publisher is an internal automation tool used by the For New Generation pop culture nostalgia channel to publish short-form video content to its own verified TikTok account @fornewgenerationtv.

The channel covers French-speaking pop culture from 1990 to 2010 — music, films, games, technology, and internet folklore. Each episode is part of a thematic "museum" series.

How it works

Content is produced through an internal production pipeline combining script writing, AI voiceover (ElevenLabs), visual assets (avatar animation + B-roll), and video assembly (FFmpeg). Once a video is approved by the channel owner, FNG Publisher uploads it via the TikTok Content Posting API to the authorized channel account.

This app does not process content from other TikTok users. It only publishes content produced and owned by the For New Generation channel.
