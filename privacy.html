Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Summary: FNG Publisher is a private internal automation tool. It does not collect, store, or process data from any user other than the single authorized channel owner. It does not interact with any third-party TikTok user data.

1. Who we are

For New Generation ("FNG", "we") is the operator of the @fornewgenerationtv TikTok channel and the FNG Publisher automation app. Contact: kaizenxhunter@gmail.com

2. What data we process

FNG Publisher processes only the following data, strictly for the purpose of publishing videos to our own authorized TikTok account:

  • OAuth access tokens issued by TikTok to the channel owner;
  • Video files produced internally by the channel;
  • Video metadata (caption, hashtags, privacy setting) entered by the channel owner.

We do not collect any data from other TikTok users, viewers, followers, commenters, or third parties.

3. How we store data

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in our internal n8n credential vault on a self-hosted server (UGREEN NAS, Tailscale private network). Video files are stored locally on the same server and are deleted after successful upload.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer any data to third parties. The only external party that receives data is TikTok itself (via the Content Posting API), which is necessary to fulfill the publishing function.

5. Data retention

  • OAuth tokens: kept until the channel owner revokes them or they expire.
  • Video metadata logs: kept for 90 days for debugging, then deleted.
  • Video files: deleted after successful upload to TikTok.

6. Your rights (GDPR / RGPD)

Since the App only processes the channel owner's own data, GDPR data-subject requests are handled internally. The channel owner can revoke OAuth access at any time via TikTok account settings.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking.

8. Security

All credentials are encrypted at rest. The server hosting the App is only reachable via a private Tailscale VPN. We follow standard security best practices (rotation of secrets, HTTPS for all OAuth callbacks, no plain-text credential storage).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The updated version is effective upon publication on this page.

10. Contact

Questions or data requests: kaizenxhunter@gmail.com
